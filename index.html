About

I am a Senior Scientist at ETH Zürich, where I have worked on next-generation Internet architecture since 2017, first as a postdoctoral researcher and senior assistant in Prof. Adrian Perrig's group.

My work centers on SCION, a path-aware Internet architecture. I led the construction of SCIONLab, a global research testbed spanning Europe, North America, Asia and Australia, and I now help grow SCIERA, a SCION-based network for research and education institutions across three continents. Around that infrastructure, my research covers secure network virtualization, global time synchronization, carbon-aware routing, programmable network telemetry, and the interplay between networking and AI.

Before ETH, I received my Ph.D., M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University, where I was advised by Prof. Heejo Lee. I also spent time as a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia.

Research

Networking for AI, AI for networking

Convergence-aware optimization of distributed AI training across data centres, ML-based prediction of path dynamics with intent-driven path selection, and sovereign, verifiable network infrastructure for federated AI.

SCIERA: SCION Education, Research and Academic Network

Building collaborations with national research and education networks, establishing a global ring connecting Asia, Europe and North America, and extending deployment to the Middle East, Africa and South America. sciera.readthedocs.io · SIGCOMM'25

Carbon-aware global routing

Estimating and disseminating the carbon intensity of inter-domain paths so that endpoints can choose lower-carbon routes. e-Energy'23

Programmable network telemetry

Next-generation debugging infrastructure with distributed data-plane operations from multiple vantage points and segment-by-segment diagnosis. ICDCS'24, ICNP'26

Global time synchronization

How precisely can clocks be synchronized on a global scale, and how can that synchronization withstand attackers trying to de-synchronize hosts and networks? SRDS'22, S&P'25

Secure inter-domain network virtualization and zoning

Virtualization architectures with security, scalability and flexibility built in, and secure zone transfer across autonomous systems running different intra-domain protocols (TRILL, SPB, VLAN, VXLAN). NDSS'20, NDSS'21

SCIONLab: a next-generation Internet testbed

A global testbed with 20 Gbps ring connectivity and points of presence in Amsterdam, Seattle, Chicago, Daejeon and Hong Kong, plus a web-based management system that lets anyone join. scionlab.org · ICNP'20 Best Paper
